# Verified Civic Resource Notes

## National Legal Services Authority

Official NALSA information states that legal services include free legal aid, legal-awareness initiatives, Lok Adalats, help accessing welfare entitlements, legal-document drafting, and legal advice. Its eligibility guidance is grounded in the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, including the Section 12 categories. The application should link citizens to the official legal-aid intake page instead of presenting eligibility as a legal determination.

- Official legal-aid page: https://nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/
- Official NALSA home page: https://nalsa.gov.in/

## RTI Online

The Department of Personnel and Training RTI Online portal supports online RTI requests and first appeals for Central Government ministries, departments, and other listed Central public authorities. Its site cautions against filing State Government authority applications through this particular portal. The application should present RTI drafts as editable assistance and direct the user to the official portal for filing.

- Official filing portal: https://rtionline.gov.in/
- Government department: Department of Personnel and Training, Government of India

## National Consumer Helpline

The Department of Consumer Affairs identifies the National Consumer Helpline as a pre-litigation grievance-redressal access point. Its official information describes multiple channels, including the web portal, the 1915 helpline, WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail, the NCH app, and UMANG. The application should use this as a direct official support route rather than portraying automated guidance as a substitute for filing a grievance.

- Official information page: https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/nch
- Official consumer support portal: https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/public/

## e-Courts

The e-Courts portal is an official judiciary resource from the e-Committee, Supreme Court of India. It presents citizen-centric services and offers access to courts, judicial-data services, and an eCourts mobile application. The product will link users outward for official case and court-service information.

- Official portal: https://ecourts.gov.in/

## myScheme

myScheme describes itself as a national one-stop search and discovery platform for Central and State/UT government schemes. It explains that citizens can enter demographic and eligibility-related details, review relevant scheme results, and navigate to the appropriate application process. The application should describe its scheme reader as orientation only and offer the official platform as the path for authoritative, current scheme criteria and applications.

- Official portal: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/

## Ministry of Labour & Employment

The Ministry of Labour & Employment describes a core responsibility to protect and safeguard the interests of workers and disadvantaged sections, as well as support a healthy work environment, skills training, and employment services. The product will connect workplace-rights users to this official source alongside its plain-language, non-advisory guidance.

- Official portal: https://labour.gov.in/
